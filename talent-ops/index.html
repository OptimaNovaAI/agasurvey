Agents

A Chat Answers. An Agent Acts.

What It Is

  • An assistant built for one defined job
  • Reads only the sources it's connected to
  • Follows set steps and can take action
  • Runs on a schedule or a trigger, as well as on request
  • Works inside the limits you set

What It Isn't

  • A smarter chat window
  • A digital employee with judgment
  • Set-and-forget
  • A way around the AI policy
  • Any better than its sources

What It Takes to Build One

  1. A job you can describe in one sentence
  2. Clean sources, and only those
  3. Permissions scoped to the job
  4. A human checkpoint
  5. A named owner
  6. Registration and approval at a tier before it runs
  7. Testing on real cases, then a review date
Green · Assisted

Retrieves, drafts, and summarizes. A person reviews everything it produces.

Yellow · Supervised

Runs routine steps. A named person approves the consequential ones. No personal information.

Red · Restricted

Acts without review, reaches outside AGA, or touches restricted data. Not permitted by default.
